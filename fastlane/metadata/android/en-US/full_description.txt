Enfócate pe causa helps you break the habit of opening social media as soon as you wake up and before you sleep. The app is in Spanish.

You choose the apps. It blocks them at night, for 45 minutes in the morning, after 15 minutes a day, and during focus sessions you can't cancel halfway. You get two 5-minute passes a day.

It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It uses the Accessibility service only to know when one of your chosen apps opens; it can't read the screen. Source code: https://github.com/michael-net/enfocate-pe-causa
